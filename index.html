01 — About

From the lab bench to the dashboard.

I started in Physics — a B.Sc. and M.Sc. from Loyola College, Chennai, including an X-ray crystallographic study that taught me how to find structure in messy, high-volume measurement data. That habit of mind, clean the inputs, test the hypothesis, trust the result, carried straight into data analytics.

Today I work with Python, SQL, Power BI, and Advanced Excel to clean datasets, surface trends, and turn raw numbers into decisions people can act on. At the Airports Authority of India, I manage and analyze large operational datasets daily.

I hold certifications from Deloitte (Forage) and Apollo Computer Education, and I bring the same rigor to a spreadsheet that I once brought to a lab bench.
